Meet Francine "Fran" Miller

Francine Miller started arrangement design out of her home garage in 1995. With a passion for sustainable local flora and community engagement, she turned her passion project into a beloved fixture of San Francisco's Mission District.

Over the past 30 years, Fran has personally mentored dozens of local florists and championed green initiatives across California flower farms.
